# TEP-019: Phase 4 Legacy UI Retirement, Responsibility-Based Structuring, Global Strict Architecture Lock, and Finalization

## Part 1: Concept & Proposal

### 1.1 Summary
This proposal specifies **Phase 4** (the final execution phase) of umbrella proposal [`TEP-015`](tep-015-ui-architecture-and-design-system-roadmap.md) following completion of Phase 3 ([`TEP-018`](tep-018-adaptive-ui-shell-and-feature-cutover.md)). Prioritizing structural cleanliness, architectural clarity, zero legacy code, and complete fulfillment of every `TEP-015` goal, it retires all `35` legacy `lib/ui/` files (`22,373` lines) and `23` legacy `test/ui/` suites plus the legacy body of `test/ui/test_helpers.dart` (`24` legacy files, `21,357` lines total); closes `2` residual error/startup parity gaps and ports `5` non-redundant behavioral integration scenarios from legacy `test/ui/` before deletion; promotes and decomposes `lib/ui_v2/` (`13` files) into a symmetric, responsibility-based `27`-file `lib/ui/` hierarchy (`lib/ui/`, `lib/ui/dialogs/`, `lib/ui/widgets/`, `lib/ui/views/`) with `P-FLATTEN-BUILD` (`< 40`-line `build()`) compliance and zero Riverpod state notifiers in `lib/ui/`; moves `ConflictDecisionsNotifier` and `RecoveryDecisionsNotifier` into `lib/presentation/sync_controller.dart`; prunes all dead Phase 1 presentation, l10n, and telemetry code, splits `board_v2_view_models.dart` by projection domain into `4` leaves, and removes every transitional `_v2`/`V2`/`.v2.`/`ui_v2` identifier, log event, and comment across `lib/` and `test/`; deletes all `81` unused legacy ARB keys (`642` to `561`) with comment-aware, member-access dead-key auditing in `audit_l10n`; locks the strict Rank `7` UI whitelist and no-provider-in-UI invariant globally in `.agents/scripts/tiertask_dev.py`; and synchronizes `spec/UI_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `README.md`, skills, and `TEP-015` to final post-migration state (`U-GATE-003`).

### 1.2 Motivation
Phase 3 (`TEP-018`) cut over `lib/main.dart` unconditionally to `TierTaskShellScreen` and `UiThemePresets`, leaving six concrete cleanup, structural-clarity, and hardening gaps required to close `TEP-015` cleanly under `P-NO-DEAD-CODE`, `P-UDF-ARCHITECTURE`, `P-FILE-SIZE`, `P-FLATTEN-BUILD`, and `U-GATE-003`:
1. **Retained Legacy UI & Test Debt:** `35` unreferenced legacy Material screens/widgets in `lib/ui/` (`22,373` lines) and `24` legacy test files in `test/ui/` (`21,357` lines, running through the test-only `LegacyTierTaskApp` wrapper) remain strictly under the bounded `TEP-015` cutover-window exception. Two behavioral parity gaps currently covered only by legacy `test/ui/home_screen_test.dart` must be fixed before deletion: `_resolveFullScreenFileReadError` leaks raw `error.toString()` instead of localized `WorkspaceException` messages on `unsupportedSettingsSchemaVersion` (and `syncNow()` fails to invalidate `activeWorkspaceProvider` on retry), and startup recovery notices are checked only once in `initState` post-frame rather than also handling async-resolved `activeWorkspaceProvider` loads.
2. **Misplaced UI State & Multi-Responsibility Catch-All Files:** `lib/ui_v2/views/resolution_views.dart` declares two Riverpod `NotifierProvider` state controllers (`conflictDecisionsProvider`/`ConflictDecisionsNotifier` and `recoveryDecisionsProvider`/`RecoveryDecisionsNotifier`) inside Rank `7` UI instead of Rank `6` `lib/presentation/`. Several promoted files bundle unrelated responsibilities (`dialogs/app_modals.dart` packs `7` distinct modals; `views/entity_section_widgets.dart` places `8` shared cross-entity widgets under `views/` instead of `widgets/`; `views/task_inline_editors.dart` packs `TaskDraftCard`, `TaskInlineTitleEditor`, and `6` section editors into `6,163` lines; `labels_view.dart` and `locations_view.dart` mix list views, entity cards, and draft cards asymmetrically with `tasks_view.dart`/`task_card.dart`; `board_v2_view_models.dart` bundles `8` providers and `30` types across `4` domains; and `68` `build()` methods in `lib/ui_v2/` exceed the `40`-line `P-FLATTEN-BUILD` threshold).
3. **Dead Phase 1 Presentation/L10n Code & Transitional `v2` Artifacts:** `lib/presentation/label_editor_controller.dart` (`181` lines) is 100% dead; `task_info_controller.dart` retains only `2` static helpers out of `390` lines; `task_editor_controller.dart` retains `13` static helpers and `2` draft resolvers while carrying ~`1,150` dead lines (including `resolvePendingHistoryCandidate` and `TaskEditorSubmitResult` subclasses); `location_editor_controller.dart` carries ~`260` dead lines of legacy CRUD/validation; `settings_controller.dart` retains unused `supportsNativeDirectoryPicker` plus trivial 1-line Phase 1 wrappers `readLogLevel` and `withLogLevel`; `ui_telemetry.dart` retains `4` dead legacy methods; `lib/l10n/tier_localization_extension.dart` retains dead `tierSectionTitle` (`6` legacy ARB keys) and uncalled `tierEmptyHint` (duplicated in `board_v2_view_models.dart`). Active files, symbols, `6` `AppLogger.event` names (`sync.v2.*`, `ui.entity_v2.persist_failed`), doc comments, and test temp prefixes still carry transitional `_v2`/`V2`/`ui_v2` names.
4. **Retired ARB Keys Without Collision-Safe Dead-Key Guard:** `81` ARB keys in `app_en.arb` and `app_de.arb` (including `themePaletteSlate`, `themePaletteNavy`, `activeFilterLabel`, the `6` `section*Tasks` keys from dead `tierSectionTitle`, legacy 3-tab editor labels/tooltips, legacy dialog headers, and unused `errorLocalFileCasExhausted`) have zero active `AppLocalizations` callers outside legacy `lib/ui/`, and `tiertask_dev.py` `audit_l10n` does not detect unused keys or orphan `@<key>` metadata blocks.
5. **Temporary Dual-Track Tooling Rules:** `.agents/scripts/tiertask_dev.py` still carries `lib/ui_v2/`/`test/ui_v2/`/`--layer ui_v2` branches, dead `settings_screen.dart` (`openSettingsFromMenu`) and `location_status_dialog` hooks, legacy `_SUBSYSTEM_TEST_MAP` pointers (`home_screen_test.dart`, `location_ui_test.dart`), stale CLI help (`lib/ui/settings_screen.dart`), and a Phase 1 `lib/ui/` check that permits `models/`/`providers/` imports and Riverpod `Notifier` declarations in `lib/ui/`. `_FORBIDDEN_UI_PROVIDERS` also lacks `windowControlRepositoryProvider` and `supportsDesktopWindowControlsProvider`.
6. **Stale Transition Documentation & Skills:** `ARCHITECTURE.md` (including `AppThemePalette.slate` at `L405`, missing `spec/UI_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` sync callout at `L4`, and `ui_v2` rows), `AGENTS.md`, `README.md` (legacy Quick Start and em dashes violating `P-LANG-RULES`), `spec/UI_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `.agents/skills/ui-design-system/SKILL.md`, `.agents/skills/red-team-audit/SKILL.md` (omitting `presentation` from DAG rank order), and `.agents/skills/update-documentation/SKILL.md` (omitting `Presentation` and `UI Component Library` layers) still contain pre-Phase-4 transition text.

### 1.4 Description

#### 1. Behavioral Regression Parity & Error-Path Hardening Before Legacy Test Deletion
Before retiring the `24` legacy `test/ui/` files, fix `2` real parity regressions in presentation controllers and port all `5` non-redundant end-to-end scenarios into `test/ui/real_mechanics_test.dart`:
1. **Local File Watcher Pause & Catch-Up (`test/ui/local_directory_picker_test.dart`):** Mount production `TierTaskApp` with a real stream-backed `LocalFileRepository`, toggle `Local Watch` off in `SettingsView`, emit external `.tdf` changes and assert the board stays unchanged while paused, then toggle `Local Watch` back on and assert immediate catch-up reconciliation.
2. **Dynamic GPS Location Condition Evaluation (`test/ui/location_ui_test.dart`):** Mount production `TierTaskApp` with a location-conditioned task, enable foreground `Location Determination` in `SettingsView`, supply a matching `LocationFix`, and assert the task moves dynamically from `Blocked` to `Focus` while the top-bar Location Status icon transitions through searching/found states.
3. **Runtime Theme Preset & Brightness Switching (`test/ui/app_theme_test.dart`):** Mount production `TierTaskApp`, toggle `Calm` vs `Plum Umber` and `Light` vs `Dark` in `SettingsView`, and assert `UiThemeTokens.of(context)` (`bg`, `panel`, `accent`, `onAccent`) updates live across views without restarting the app.
4. **Localized Settings Schema Error & Workspace Retry (`test/ui/home_screen_test.dart`):** Update `_resolveFullScreenFileReadError` in `tasks_view_model.dart` to localize `WorkspaceException` via `localizations.localizeWorkspaceError(error)` (replacing raw `error?.toString()`, upholding `P-ERR-PRESERVE`), update `SyncController.syncNow()` to invalidate `activeWorkspaceProvider` when `activeWorkspaceProvider.hasError`, and assert `TierTaskApp` renders localized `unsupportedSettingsSchemaVersion` full-screen error diagnostics and recovers on `Retry`.
5. **Early & Async Startup Recovery Notice (`test/ui/home_screen_test.dart`):** Update `FloatingNoticeController` to consume any pending one-shot settings recovery notice (`recoveredFromBackup`, `resetAfterQuarantine`) both when `activeWorkspaceProvider` is already resolved on first frame and when it transitions asynchronously from `AsyncLoading` to `AsyncData`, and assert persistent toast display in `TierTaskApp` (plus unit coverage in `test/presentation/floating_notice_controller_test.dart`).
